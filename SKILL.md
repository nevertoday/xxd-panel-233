## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a handmade tufted textile wall hanging: thick loop-pile yarn, soft relief, imperfect edges, naïve rounded forms and a few memorable fibre symbols. The ground is an extremely pale flat colour matched to the source temperature—warm white, cream, mist grey, pale pink-white or pale blue-white—with no room, furniture or texture. Subjects stay small, sparse and asymmetric inside vast negative space. Colours are the brightest, most living hues from the source, raised in clarity: sky blue, lake teal, fresh green, warm yellow, coral, peach. It is not a grey Morandi filter, a photographic textile mockup, a filled wall, a cheap candy palette or a commercial sticker sheet.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
